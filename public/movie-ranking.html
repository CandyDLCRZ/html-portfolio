The Best Series According to Katrin Erika

My top 3 favorite Series of all time.

House of The Dragons

I love *House of the Dragon* because it brings the world of Westeros to life in such a visually stunning way. The graphics, cinematography, costumes, sets, and especially the dragon scenes are beautifully crafted and make every episode feel grand and immersive. The actors also add so much depth to the series, with powerful performances that make the characters feel complex, emotional, and believable. Their chemistry and ability to portray ambition, loyalty, love, and conflict make every scene more captivating. What I love most, however, is the storyline. It is filled with political intrigue, family conflicts, unexpected twists, and powerful characters fighting for what they believe is rightfully theirs. The story keeps me curious about what will happen next while also showing the complicated relationships within the Targaryen family. Overall, *House of the Dragon* combines breathtaking visuals, outstanding acting, and a compelling storyline in a way that makes it incredibly exciting and enjoyable to watch.

The Crown

I love this series. It brings back so many memories about the British Royal Family. I am always fascinated about how the Monarchy works and on how King & Queens, Prince & Princesses actually live their lives. Although they say that The Crown is just a fiction and not based on real life, but watching this somehow gives you a glimpse of how living as a Royal would look like.

Suits

Now this one is different from the first 2 series I've mentioned above. It is an American Series about Corporate lawyers in Manhattan. I love this show because its really funny and the actors are very good in portraying their roles.
